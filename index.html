Why this exists

The failure mode

Drawing a rectangle or a black box over text hides it visually. The bytes stay in the file. Press Select All, copy, or run pdftotext — the "redacted" text comes right back.

It has happened to professionals with real stakes: black-box redactions were reversed by copy-paste in Apple v. Samsung, in Meta's 2025 FTC antitrust filings (journalists recovered data about Apple, Snap and Google), and in court filings that drew sanctions — one Florida firm was reportedly fined $30,000.

Limitations — read before you rely on this

  • Text only. This checks the text layer, metadata, and raw file bytes. It cannot see inside images or scanned pages.
  • "Not found" is strong evidence, not proof. Exotic encodings or OCR-only content can hide strings from any extractor. For court-level assurance, also open the file, Select All, and try to copy the redacted area.
  • Not legal advice. Rules differ by court and jurisdiction (e.g. the District of Minnesota's redaction best practices and the US Court of Federal Claims guidance).
  • Real redaction means deleting content, not covering it. If this tool says FAIL, rebuild the page or use a tool that removes the text (Acrobat's Redact panel, or rasterize the page to an image).
